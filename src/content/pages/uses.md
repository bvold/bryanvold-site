title: "Uses"

The digital tools, analog essentials, and little comforts that make up my everyday writing setup.

## Digital

- **Laptop:** Dell Pro Max 16 (32 GB RAM, Arc GPU)
- **Operating system:** Windows 11 Pro or Kubuntu 26.04
- **Keyboard:** Logitech MX Keys S (wireless)
- **Displays:** Dual-monitor setup (Dell 20-inch and Samsung 28-inch 4K)
- **Smartphone:** Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra

## Analog

- **Journaling:** Soft-cover A5 suede notebook
- **Creative writing:** Hard-cover blue A5 notebook
- **Novel drafting:** Hard-cover red A5 notebook
- **Pens:** Pentel EnerGel (0.5 mm), Sharpie S-Gel (0.7 mm), Zebra Sarasa Clip (0.5 mm), and Platinum Preppy (experimenting)

## Vibes

- **Coffee:** Bulletproof coffee, brewed in a French press (16 oz)
- **Lighting:** Natural light or incandescent
- **Sound:** Headphones with music, ambient rain, trance—or silence
