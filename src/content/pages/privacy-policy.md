*Last updated: November 7, 2026*

Your privacy matters to me. This is a personal website, and I've kept it simple on purpose: it collects as little as possible, and nothing is sold, shared, or used to track you.

## The short version

- The only personal information I receive is what you choose to send through the [Contact](/contact/) form.
- I use it only to read and reply to your message.
- This site has no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking cookies.

## The contact form

When you send a message, I receive your **name**, **email address**, the **topic** you picked, and your **message**. It arrives in my personal email inbox, and I use it only to read and respond to you. I won't add you to a mailing list, share your details with anyone, or use them for anything else.

Messages stay in my email the way any correspondence does. If you'd like me to delete a message you sent, just ask and I will.

## Spam protection

The Contact page uses **Cloudflare Turnstile** to tell people apart from spam bots. It looks at technical signals from your browser to do this; it isn't used for advertising or to follow you around the web. You can read [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.

## Hosting

This site is hosted by **Cloudflare**. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. I don't use that information to identify or track visitors.

## What this site doesn't do

- No advertising, and no ad or tracking cookies
- No analytics or visitor tracking
- No selling or sharing of your information

The site's search runs entirely in your browser, so what you search for isn't sent to me.

## Links to other sites

Links to other websites, including my Instagram, Pinterest, and Facebook pages, take you to sites with their own privacy practices.

## Changes

If anything here changes (for example, if I start a newsletter), I'll update this page and the date at the top.

## Questions

If you have any questions about your privacy or want a message deleted, please [get in touch](/contact/).
